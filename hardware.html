Camera box: from design to the sprayer

Designing, printing, assembling, wiring and mounting the camera boxes used on the boom.

Step 1

Design

Enclosure insert modeled in CAD to hold every component in a fixed position.

↓
Step 2

3D printing

Insert printed and lined with 3M VHB tape to lock the components in place.

↓
Step 3

Enclosure machining

Openings cut in the weatherproof box for the camera, connectors and cabling.

↓
Step 4

Component assembly

Camera, Actuonix linear actuator, fan and terminal blocks fitted into the insert.

↓
Step 5

Wiring & soldering

Power and signal wiring soldered and routed out through a sealed Deutsch connector.

↓
Step 6

Bracket fabrication

Steel brackets drilled and bolted to clamp the box onto the boom frame.

↓
Step 7

Mounting on the boom

Box hung under the boom, with the camera facing down at the crop.

↓
Step 8

On the sprayer

Final installation on the commercial sprayer, ready for field trials.
